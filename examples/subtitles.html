Subtitles & captions

Drop in <track> children exactly like native <video>. Lumen auto-detects them, remembers the viewer's last choice, and renders cues through its own overlay so styling (size, color, background, edge, position, timing offset) is fully under your control — open the Settings → Captions → Appearance menu to try it.

Styling from JS

player.setSubtitlePrefs({
  fontSize: 1.3,
  color: "#fff",
  background: "#000",
  backgroundOpacity: 0.7,
  edge: "outline",
  position: "top",
  offsetSeconds: 0.5, // shift cues later by 500ms
});
