HLS (adaptive bitrate)

Multiple renditions, automatic quality switching, and a manual quality menu (Settings → Quality) driven by the HLS manifest.

Quality levels and the active rendition are exposed on the element: 

const player = document.querySelector("lumen-player");
player.on("qualitieschange", ({ levels }) => console.log(levels));
player.on("qualitychange", ({ level, auto }) => console.log(level, auto));
player.setQuality("auto"); // or a specific level id
